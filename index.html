Cast Acrylic (PMMA)

Category:
Thermoplastic
Properties:
High impact resistance, excellent optical clarity, easily machined and laser-cut.
Common Uses:
Signage, point-of-sale displays, protective enclosures.
Sustainability:
Recycling Code 7. Can be recycled, but facilities are limited compared to PET/HDPE.

European Oak

Category:
Hardwood
Properties:
Dense, durable, attractive grain pattern, resistant to fungal attacks.
Common Uses:
High-end indoor furniture, flooring, architectural joinery.
Sustainability:
Renewable resource if FSC certified. Fully biodegradable.

Aluminium (6082-T6)

Category:
Non-Ferrous Metal
Properties:
Excellent strength-to-weight ratio, high corrosion resistance, easy to machine.
Common Uses:
Structural framing, automotive parts, custom machined enclosures.
Sustainability:
100% recyclable without loss of properties. High energy required for initial extraction.
