Cast Acrylic (PMMA)

Category:
Thermoplastic
Properties:
High impact resistance, excellent optical clarity, easily machined and laser-cut.
Common Uses:
Signage, point-of-sale displays, protective enclosures.
Sustainability:
Recycling Code 7. Can be recycled, but facilities are limited compared to PET/HDPE.

European Oak

Category:
Hardwood
Properties:
Dense, durable, attractive grain pattern, resistant to fungal attacks.
Common Uses:
High-end indoor furniture, flooring, architectural joinery.
Sustainability:
Renewable resource if FSC certified. Fully biodegradable.

Aluminium (6082-T6)

Category:
Non-Ferrous Metal
Properties:
Excellent strength-to-weight ratio, high corrosion resistance, easy to machine.
Common Uses:
Structural framing, automotive parts, custom machined enclosures.
Sustainability:
100% recyclable without loss of properties. High energy required for initial extraction.

MDF (Medium-Density Fibreboard)

Category:
Manufactured Board
Properties:
Smooth finish without natural grain, uniform structure, easily CNC-routed and painted. Vulnerable to water unless sealed.
Common Uses:
Flat-pack furniture, internal architectural mouldings, prototyping models.
Sustainability:
Made from recycled wood fibers, but resins/glues make it difficult to recycle at end-of-life.

Corrugated Cardboard

Category:
Paper-Based Composite
Properties:
Lightweight, high strength-to-weight ratio due to fluted inner layer, easily cut and folded.
Common Uses:
Packaging boxes, structural protective padding, rapid prototyping.
Sustainability:
Widely recycled globally, biodegradable, and often produced from high percentages of recycled pulp.

Copper

Category:
Non-Ferrous Metal
Properties:
Exceptional electrical and thermal conductivity, highly ductile, antimicrobial, forms a protective green patina over time.
Common Uses:
Electrical wiring, heat exchangers, plumbing pipes, decorative architectural elements.
Sustainability:
Infinitely recyclable with zero loss of quality; high scrap value drives high recycling rates.

PLA (Polylactic Acid)

Category:
Bioplastic / Thermoplastic
Properties:
Easy to 3D print (low warping), rigid, relatively brittle, low heat tolerance (~60°C softening point).
Common Uses:
3D printing filament, disposable food packaging, medical implants.
Sustainability:
Derived from renewable resources (corn starch/sugar cane). Industrially compostable, but will not easily degrade in standard landfill environment.
